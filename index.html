The problem

Carbon is counted in gigatonnes.
People are counted in nothing.

0.0 Gt

CO₂ traded in the voluntary market — measured, priced, audited to the tonne.

?

The jobs, schools, clinics and clean cookstoves those projects create — almost never measured.

A tonne avoided in Kenya and a tonne avoided in Norway are priced the same. The lives they change are not — so capital flows to the cheapest tonne, not the greatest good.
